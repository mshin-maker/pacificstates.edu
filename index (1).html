    About the University

    About Pacific States University

    Education is the Best Investment

    Pacific States University (PSU) carries a 100-year legacy in STEM education, tracing its origins to 1928, when William G. Buffinger founded the Radio Institute of California in Los Angeles. Evolving from its pioneering work in radio and electronic engineering, PSU has continually advanced to encompass computer science, data science, and business technology. Today, as a private, non-profit university, PSU remains dedicated to STEM-based, research-oriented education, preparing students with the analytical and technological skills needed for success in modern global industries.
