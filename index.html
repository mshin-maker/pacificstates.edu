    Stay on track

    • Attend at least 80% of class hours — below that is an automatic F.
    • Keep a 2.0 GPA (undergrad) or 3.0 (grad).
    • Pass at least 67% of attempted units each quarter.

    Get recognized

    • Dean's List — 3.7+ GPA in a full-time quarter.
    • President's List — 4.0 GPA in a full-time quarter.
    • Top 3 graduates earn Latin honors.

    Changed your mind?

    • Cancel for a full refund through the first class or day 7 after enrolling, whichever is later.
    • Pro-rata refund if you withdraw before 60% of the quarter.
    • Email registrar@psuca.edu — any written notice works.
